Collection methods and limits

Scope

Six public officials across three states, ten issue categories inherited from the existing Chhattisgarh OMS, within 5 August–5 November 2026 inclusive. Records capture press coverage, reported public actions, decisions and attributed statements. The archive is incomplete and does not imply a comprehensive retrieval of every item in every edition.

Evidence hierarchy

1. Dated publisher article and original URL. 2. Individual social-media post URL corroborated by a publisher's embed. 3. Publicly identifiable profile. 4. Date- and issue-specific discovery search URL. Only categories 1 and 2 are item-level evidence, and neither certifies independently that reported claims are true.

Issue classifications

Primary topic is assigned using the article's focus. An allegation is recorded as an attributed allegation, an announcement as an announcement, and a predicted outcome as a projection. “Internal Security & Bastar” is the existing cross-state category; in Madhya Pradesh, Odisha and Jharkhand it means internal security, Maoist activity, policing and rehabilitation, not a claim that the event occurred in Bastar.

Blocked publishers

Dainik Bhaskar is retained as a headline-discovery source where direct automated article access is restricted. A discovered headline should not be counted as fully read coverage, and no access restrictions are bypassed. Edition-based PDFs/ePapers are tracked separately from extractable article pages.

Social / LinkedIn / Meta

X post URLs recovered from newspaper embeds are clickable; availability at X varies. Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn are represented through public profiles and prefilled issue searches, but a search result is never presented as an authenticated individual post. No historical Meta or LinkedIn API export has been imported.

Coverage and chronology

Publication dates are used for filtering. Reported actions may have occurred earlier. Source frequency is affected by publisher availability, translation, duplicate stories and targeted research time. Do not interpret item counts as relative activity, media favourability or policy effectiveness.
