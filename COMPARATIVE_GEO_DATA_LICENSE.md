# Comparative district-boundary data — provenance and licence

The three geographic layers displayed in the Madhya Pradesh–Odisha–Jharkhand comparative political-intelligence dashboard have **actual district polygon geometry**, not invented cartograms.

- Data source: [Vardhan Systems / vardhan-maps](https://github.com/Vardhan-Systems/vardhan-maps), district boundaries derived from OpenStreetMap.
- Required attribution: [© OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- Boundary data licence: [Open Database License v1.0 (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
- Included states: Madhya Pradesh (55 polygons), Odisha (30), Jharkhand (24).
- Geometry files: `comparative-geo-mp.json`, `comparative-geo-od.json`, `comparative-geo-jh.json`.
- Bundled client-side database: `comparative-geo-data.js`, generated from the three data files; it is also subject to ODbL.
- Transformation applied: removed unused geographic feature properties and retained district names and polygon coordinates; no invented state or district outlines were added.
- The data is for exploratory research visualisation. It is not Survey of India certified, is not suitable for land/legal boundary determination, and should not be interpreted as precise event locations.

**Source-to-district highlighting** matches names and declared place-name aliases in research records. A location in a title or report is evidence of a mention, **not** by itself proof that an event occurred there. Politicians' records, institutional government orders, and Vidhan Sabha reports have separate provenance labels. Missing district matches remain neutral.

The publisher/source ledger is compiled separately and is not claimed to be covered by OpenStreetMap or Vardhan maps licensing; its original articles, links and platform media remain with the respective rightsholders.
